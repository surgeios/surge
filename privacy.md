Privacy Policy for SURGE: for Plex
Last updated: October 11, 2026

SURGE: for Plex does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

SURGE connects to your Plex account and your Plex servers so you can browse and play your own media. Your sign-in token and settings are stored only on your device. This traffic goes directly between your device and Plex or your servers; it is never passed elsewhere. Plex’s handling of your data is covered by Plex’s privacy policy.

Purchases: Subscriptions and purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I don’t receive your payment information or Apple ID details.

Analytics and tracking: SURGE contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children: The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
